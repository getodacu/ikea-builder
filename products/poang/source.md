## Mesh and verification limits

Original procedural Three.js model and original vector gallery; no IKEA page or image incorporated into either. Coordinates in mm, +X viewer's right, +Y up, +Z front, floor origin below nominal center. The US article sets the envelope; bends, sections, thicknesses, offsets, recesses, hardware lengths and animation travel are schematic, not a measured fit or furniture specification. The two illustrated “first/other” sides are unlabeled; the model fixes the first as left viewed from the front. Receiver stations are handed to match, not separately selectable. Work poses show the two back-stile faces, a first arm attachment and a turn to the second. The upright Complete pose reuses the final installed frame, rather than adding a numbered turn or claiming to show numerical torque. The tab openings and screw/receiver centerlines are represented; threads, flexibility, textile stretch, loads and tightening torque are not simulated.
