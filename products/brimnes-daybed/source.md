## Dimensions and fidelity

The US article gives a compact length × width × height of 76¾ × 41 × 22⅞ in., an extended bed width of 78 in., and inside drawer width × depth × height of 31½ × 21¼ × 6¼ in. The model uses millimetres, floor origin under the compact frame, +X along the length, +Y up and +Z out of the drawers. Panel thickness, bores, rail sections, clearances, cam travel, staging and camera paths are illustrative, not IKEA engineering dimensions. Meshes and transparent gallery linework are original procedural drawings, not scans or traced page assets. No mechanical fit, load or physical assembly is certified.
