- [IKEA US desk combination 995.528.65](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/p/relatera-desk-sit-stand-white-s99552865/) lists two packages: [sit/stand underframe 805.403.06](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/p/relatera-underframe-sit-stand-f-table-top-white-80540306/) and [46⅛ × 23⅝-in. tabletop 305.402.95](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/p/relatera-table-top-white-30540295/). The combination and underframe link the same [US assembly PDF](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/assembly_instructions/relatera-underframe-sit-stand-f-table-top-white__AA-2396864-4-100.pdf). The tabletop article has no separate assembly booklet; it identifies the underframe as the source of the caps and the through holes as accessory mounts.
- Edition `AA-2396864-4-100`, printed footer `AA-2396864-4`, dated 2024-09-18 on p. 20. `shasum -a 256` on the downloaded PDF returned `3e7760cc3a9d1f3be9b003310d0af0d22a40c2195760d7abdcce4babfa9d1868`; `pdfinfo` returned 20 pages. Inspected the Poppler renders of all 20 pages, including the diagrams, arrows and part callouts. All pages below are one-based.
- P. 1 depicts both widths. P. 2 requires a cross-head screwdriver, a spirit level, two people and a protected surface; pp. 3–4 inventory the fittings and width-specific cap use. P. 5 selects the wide desk (1200 mm width class in the shared booklet); the narrower 900 mm alternative figures 1–2 on p. 6 and three-cap figure 17 on p. 17 do not apply to this article. P. 12 shows the two crank positions. P. 18 shows leveling the same four glides with a spirit level, lifting the desk to adjust. P. 19 shows unlocking, unfolding, cranking and parking the handle. P. 20 depicts distributing loads, not concentrating them. These last three pages are references, not numbered assembly actions.
- The US combination article says not to place the desk near blinds, stairs or anything else that may endanger a child; always cover each accessory hole with the included caps or compatible RELATERA accessories to reduce finger entrapment. The article limits evenly distributed weight to 110 lb, concentrated loads on a small surface to 33 lb, and the hook to 11 lb. No above-table accessories are needed for the modeled configuration.

## Geometry and evidence limits

Original Three.js geometry and original SVG gallery composition; no IKEA images or renders ship with the product. Millimetres, +X viewer right, +Y up, +Z front, origin beneath the center. The combined US article's Measurements and dimension drawing specify a 1172 × 600 mm tabletop, 19 mm thickness (component article), roughly 1280 mm overall width, 581 mm underframe depth, and 651–1080 mm working height. The combination prose also gives 65–108 cm; the component prose lists a different 27½–47¼ in., while the component's Measurements specify 25¼–41¾ in. for the bare frame. The assembled measurements/dimension drawing control this model. Bore spacing, channel section, fastener outlines, fits and locking slide distances are diagram estimates, not fabrication dimensions. The geometry shows the moving upper column jackets attached to the top carriage as in p. 19 and keeps both lower/base profiles stationary. The demonstration unlocks and unfolds the handle, then turns it as both leg carriages rise together by a representative 180 mm; the shown relation is not a turns-to-height calibration, load test, or full-travel claim. The guide does not replace the official safety or use instructions.
