## Stock and exclusions

- Article: three retail packages; the booklet does not map fittings to individual carton numbers. Pages 4 and 5 are the two separately illustrated groups in the reader. Loose boards are identified by the assembly figures, not assigned IKEA hardware numbers.
- P. 4: `100214` ×12, `109029` ×10, `148510` ×2, `105344` ×3, `100344` ×21, `100365` ×12, `118331 / 112996` ×6 (alternate markings in steps 2 and 42, not extra parts), `103430` ×4, `119030` ×2, `101350` ×28, `101345` ×2, `110519` ×2, `101449` ×5, `103095` ×5, supplied key `100001` ×1, `101532` ×12, `107091` ×4 (individual dots on peel-off carriers).

The bag and rail set are split into their physical members in the viewer; no bag or duplicate rail set is installed.
- Unnumbered: two handed end panels, one base, four divider uprights and two depth crosspieces, two upper lengthwise rails, one front plinth, one partial back, one worktop, three shelves, two tall doors, one short drawer-bay door, drawer front, two sides, a back and a bottom (25 wooden parts).
- Step 9 has an internal count conflict: the p. 10 inset prints `6×`, while the two posts in the main figure each visibly have **two** dowels at each end (eight total). Those eight pictured bores also exactly reconcile the p. 4 stock of 28 with the twenty dowels called out elsewhere. Viewer shows the pictured pairs, not six arbitrarily selected holes. Step 53 uses two of four supplied bumper dots; the two others remain unused on every route. The one supplied key is a tool, not installed. No supplied item is removed and no other stock is route-exclusive.
- Bring the p. 3 tools: slotted, cross-head and round-symbol driver, hammer, level, pencil and clamps. Use two people and a protected assembly surface, as pictured. Pp. 2, 4 and 31 require wall anchoring with the supplied devices; wall screws and plugs are not supplied. Suitable fixings depend on the wall; if uncertain seek professional advice. Four abstract wall screws in the model are separate requirements, not a fixing recommendation or inclusion of plugs. Sink, strainer/stopper, faucet, knobs/handles and optional organizers are sold separately. No plumbing, countertop cutting, appliances or adjacent cabinet are modeled.
