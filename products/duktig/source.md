## Setup and inventory

- P. 2 (translations pp. 3–5): serious or fatal crushing injuries can occur from tip-over. Always use the supplied wall attachment device. Wall screws and plugs are not included; select hardware suitable for the wall and seek professional advice if unsure. Pp. 7 and 25 repeat the wall-attachment pictogram. IKEA’s US article specifies adult assembly, a small-parts hazard before assembly, and no use with water or real food.
- P. 6: two people; protect the surface; bring a cross-head screwdriver, pencil, ruler and hammer; ask IKEA if unsure. Supplied 100006 key is a tool, not installed. The hammer is called out for 148819, and the faucet drawing specifically rules out a power driver. Wall-specific tools are outside this guide.
- Pp. 7–8: 2 loose modules (152849 faucet and 150916 housed cooktop), 119 numbered pieces from the packet and loose fitting drawings, and 20 unnumbered pieces below. Total 141 supplied including one tool, 140 installed. One generic wall-fixing marker represents externally sourced wall hardware; it does not imply a single specified screw/plug set or verify any particular wall. The four batteries and the ordinary hand tools are not in the model inventory.
- Packet inset: 128814 cross-head fixing screw ×25, 128815 catch ×3. Loose fittings: 100224 long hex-key screw ×10; 100295 short hex-key screw ×14; 100226 handle screw ×6; 109597 faucet screw ×2; 109041 strap screw ×1; 101344 dowel ×17; 108768 strike plate ×3; 118562 shelf support ×8; 100823 washer ×5; 148819 cross-bore worktop connector ×4; 118678 hook ×5; 118679 hook rail ×1; 120104 pull ×3; 107967 wall strap ×1; 118650 pivot bracket ×6; 100006 key ×1; 147536 foot sleeve ×4. - Unnumbered lower stock: two preassembled side/leg units, base, front rail, center divider, two shelves, back and two doors (acrylic-window and solid). Unnumbered upper stock: two tall side panels, shelf, two dividers (one takes the catch), back, cap, acrylic-window door. Worktop and removable sink are the remaining unnumbered pieces. - P. 9 distinguishes starting with the lower unit (left drawing, step 1) and jumping to p. 14 (right drawing); this guide follows the complete printed 1–46 route, not a second assembly of either module. P. 12 shows three leg-height configurations (bare legs, sleeves, reversed sleeves); the illustrated model seats the four sleeves in one configuration. It doesn’t predict the clearances or height at the other settings.
