## References, geometry and verification

- Page 59 shows a 38¼ × 74½ in. (97 × 189 cm) mattress and forbids its top rising above the marked line. The US article rounds the mattress length differently (74⅜ in.); the figure's metric length is 189 cm. The article lists an 8¼-in. maximum mattress thickness, a minimum 94½-in. ceiling, no use of the upper bed by under-six children, and no added hooks/handles or hanging rope/belts. Use the physical line and the official instructions; the studio does not validate fit, loading, clearance, handling, or fastener strength.
- The finished right-handed datum is floor centre, +x viewer right, +y up, +z front. Outer extent is based on the article's 77½ × 71⅝ × 44⅞ in. width × height × depth, and loft underside on 55⅞ in. Thicknesses, relative coordinates, accessory profiles, and tool scale are schematic estimates traced by visual relationships, not measured tolerances. All shipped meshes are original procedural constructions; there are no IKEA assets bundled. Static storage is schematic and differs visually by the p. 6 choice; the main-frame top receiver patterns are common to upper/lower for the same side (pp. 17/19 and 43/45) and handed between sides (pp. 21/47). Eased stiles and real grip cutouts are distinct from the tread holes; rails have opposing clip lips and the raised-frame joints have separate sockets. Hardware textures and some tiny pilot holes are simplified. Build motions illustrate relative alignment, not a validation of collision-free human handling.
