## Fidelity and verification

The original gallery illustration and original procedural white boards, runner openings, shaped fronts and fitting meshes use no IKEA artwork or redistributed source. Millimetres, +X right, +Y up, +Z front; assembled floor origin at center. Drawer face heights, drill stations, runner channel cross sections, open extension and turning paths are schematic measurements taken from relative figure positions, not published machining instructions. The model shows the mounting relationship, not a detailed or load-calibrated reconstruction of the ball bearing/slide mechanism. Official PDF and Poppler renders are not part of the product assets.
