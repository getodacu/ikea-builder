# FJÄLLBO source ledger

- Exact variant: black 78-in. TV unit, US article 005.790.34. The [official US article](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/p/fjaellbo-tv-unit-black-00579034/) links the [assembly PDF](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/assembly_instructions/fjaellbo-tv-unit-black__AA-2503161-1-100.pdf), describes powder-coated steel, a solid pine top/shelf, an open back, adjustable feet and adhesive cable clips. Published envelope: 78 × 17¾ × 21¼ in. (1981.2 × 450.85 × 539.75 mm), not the package dimensions. IKEA specifies a 90 lb maximum TV weight and 110 lb maximum shelf load; consult the official article for TV placement guidance.
- File edition `AA-2503161-1-100`, printed footer `AA-2503161-1`, date 2024-04-24 (p. 32). `shasum -a 256 manual.pdf`: `a1a35b11570f06b080e3c5146f379d44087d7876cb98da008d364ebc4ab9ec13`; `pdfinfo manual.pdf`: 32 pages. Inspected the rendered pp. 1–32 and the detail figures at original resolution. Pages here are one-based PDF pages.
- P. 1 cover; pp. 2–4 multilingual warning: serious or fatal crushing injuries can occur from tip-over, use the provided attachment devices, use wall screws/plugs suitable for the actual wall (not included), seek professional advice when unsure. P. 5 shows cross-head and slotted screwdrivers, a spirit level, two people and protecting the work surface.
