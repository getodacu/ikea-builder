# IKEA assembly authoring

Invocation example: “Add an assembly for [product and variant] from [official IKEA manual URL].”

Work from the ikea-builder root. Treat the guide as a supplement to the official instructions. On revisions, apply the same gates to the changed steps and every route that reaches them.

1. **Pin and read the edition.** Record the exact variant, locale, official IKEA PDF URL, edition, PDF page count, and SHA-256. Use visual renders to inspect numbered figures, insets, arrows, handed orientation, tips, tools, two-person setup, and anchoring warnings; extracted text is only an index. Write `products/<slug>/source.md` with the one-based PDF page, printed number, new quantities, reuse, and adjustments for every figure. Separate setup and reference-only pages from the numbered sequence. Use `products/malsjo/source.md` and `products/malsjo/manual.js` for the ledger and edition/page map. Stop at the exact page and figure if edition, attachment, or safety-critical evidence is missing, unreadable, or conflicting; resolve it before publishing the affected instructions.
2. **Reconcile the physical stock.** Use `products/malsjo/kit.js` for family and instance IDs. Reconcile packets, loose fittings, unnumbered panels, alternate part numbers, supplied tools, external accessories, optional pieces, and route exclusions. Reuse the same instance IDs when hardware or whole subassemblies move; account for placements, reuse, removals and adjustments separately. MALSJÖ's `118331 / 112956` is one alternate-number family, the key is supplied but wall fixings are external, and a no-skirting route leaves two spacers unused. These are evidence examples, not stock for another product. Gate authoring on all supplied quantities and per-route exclusions balancing.
3. **Author operations and verify mating geometry.** Declare units, axes, origin, published or measured dimensions, and schematic estimates. Specify participants, prerequisites, mating faces and bores, before/after poses, rigid moves, and captive adjustments. Consult `products/malsjo/kit.js` for the operation helpers and case, `products/malsjo/doors.js` and `products/malsjo/drawers.js` for handed and rigid placements, and `products/malsjo/model.js` for selecting records in printed order. Use `products/malsjo/geometry.js` and `products/malsjo/joinery.js` for original meshes and receiver relationships. Verify a handed orientation and an actual fastener/receiver against independent figures; raycast important openings in the rendered mesh where appropriate, as in `products/malsjo/geometry.test.js`. Ship only original or redistribution-authorized assets with provenance and required notices. Keep source PDF pages and renders outside the build. Document physical uncertainty separately from camera and motion choices; the guide certifies neither fit, clearance, anchoring, nor load.
4. **Register one product.** Put `manual.js`, `product.js`, geometry, `source.md`, and product tests under `products/<slug>/`. Use `products/malsjo/product.js` for reader data: only this edition's inventory groups, chapters, unboxing, warnings, references, and choices. Keep warnings with their instructions and link to their official PDF pages. Use `src/timeline.js` for deterministic playback and `src/guide.js` and `src/scene.js` for the shared reader and 3D view. Follow `products/malsjo/scene.js` for the lazy scene factory interface; an ordinary linear product needs only geometry, no presentation hooks or choice gate. Register the slug once in `products/catalog.js` with an explicit product import, manual and preview pages. Use `npm run manual:prepare -- <slug>` (Poppler and IKEA HTTPS) for verified local previews. The existing `npm run manual:prepare -- malsjo` uses the same cache interface; no source media goes in `public/` or `dist/`. Use the worked files directly, not a scaffold or a copy of the reader.
5. **Gate delivery on evidence.** On Node 24, run `npm ci`, `npm test`, `npm run build`, then `npm run preview -- --host 127.0.0.1 --port 5198 --strictPort`; open the catalog and the selected `?product=<slug>` link. Use `products/malsjo/model.test.js` and `products/malsjo/geometry.test.js` for checks of all printed steps, reconciled route inventory, repeatable replay/back/seek boundary poses and IDs, and keyed joints. In the browser inspect and replay a part, go back and return, open and close a source preview without seeking, exercise keyboard/mobile and reduced motion, and block 3D and thumbnail requests to verify text-only navigation and official links. Confirm `dist/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` exists and `dist/` contains no PDF pages or PNG renders. Record the edition, commands, observed versus expected results, unexercised paths, and fidelity or physical limits separately in the product ledger.
